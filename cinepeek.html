01 CONTEXT
THE PROBLEM

What needed to work better?

Movie and show discovery needs to work across content types while bringing external TMDb data into the app. Watchlists also need to persist without a database.

THE SOLUTION

A focused product experience

CinePeek combines search and discovery flows in an Express app, with filters for genre, language and media type, a random-pick feature, and JSON-file watchlist storage.

KEY FEATURES

Flexible discovery

Explore movies and shows by genre, language and media type through one streaming-inspired interface.

02

Use a surprise selection when you want the app to choose a title to explore.

03

Persistent watchlist

Save titles to a watchlist that uses JSON-file storage rather than a database.

TECHNICAL CHALLENGE

Connect TMDb content, discovery flows and watchlist persistence in one Express app.

APPROACH

The app uses Express and EJS for its experience, Axios and the TMDb API for content, and JSON-file storage for the watchlist.

03 RESULTS & TAKEAWAYS
WHAT IT DELIVERS

From idea to usable experience

CinePeek delivers a responsive dark interface with movie and TV discovery, random picks and watchlist features.

PROJECT TAKEAWAYS
  • The discovery flow supports both movies and shows.
  • A lightweight JSON file stores the watchlist without a database.
  • Content discovery and personal saving sit together in one app.
